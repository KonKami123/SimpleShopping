Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

SimpleShopping — Focus Shopper & Deal Checker is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains in plain English how the browser extension handles data.

1. Local-Only Webpage Processing

SimpleShopping only runs when you explicitly click the extension's toolbar icon. It reads product and search listing details from the active webpage locally inside your browser to generate your Deal Verdict and red-flag checks.

2. No Browsing History Collection

  • SimpleShopping never collects, tracks, sells, or transmits your browsing history, page URLs, or shopping activity to external servers.
  • There are no third-party analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or background data harvesters in the extension.

3. Local-Only 30-Day Price History & Preferences

Your 30-day price history (stored under ssPriceHistory) and your display/voice preferences—such as Dark Slate or Soft Light mode, custom text colours, text size, Dyslexia Spacing, Reading Spotlight, Verdict Tone (Quirky or Straight to the Point), Read Aloud voice/speed/volume, and Pro unlock status—are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local. Price history is automatically pruned after 30 days and never leaves your browser.

4. Click-Only Comparison Links & Affiliate Disclosure

SimpleShopping offers optional buttons to check product images on Google Lens and compare prices on eBay UK, Argos, Currys, and Amazon UK. Comparison URLs are only resolved and opened when you explicitly click a button—no passive affiliate tags are injected into pages you browse.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. When you explicitly click the "Compare on Amazon UK" button, the opened search link includes our partner tag (simplebrowsin-21) at no extra cost to you. No personal data from SimpleShopping is sent to any retailer; each retailer's own privacy policy applies only if you choose to click through and visit their site.

5. SimpleShopping Pro Licence Verification

If you choose to purchase the optional £3 one-time SimpleShopping Pro upgrade via ExtensionPay, your paid status is verified and stored locally in chrome.storage.local so your Pro themes remain unlocked.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or need help using SimpleShopping, please contact support@simplebrowsing.app.
