Calm, Scam-Free Shopping Guide

Modern shopping sites pack fake countdowns, inflated RRPs, and paid sponsored ads onto every screen. A few quick checks help you shop with confidence:

  • Watch Out for Keyboard-Smash Brands: Random all-caps names paired with 5-star reviews from a handful of buyers are often generic dropshipped items. Use "Check Photo on Google Lens" to see where else the exact photo appears.
  • Question Huge RRP Discounts: A 60%+ discount off an inflated "Was" price does not always mean a real bargain—check your 30-day tracked price history and compare across Argos, Currys, and eBay UK.
  • Filter Out Sponsored Clutter: On search pages, SimpleShopping groups clean organic results under "Worth a Look" and separates paid ads and red-flag listings under "Skip It".
  • Listen at Your Own Pace: Click "Read Verdict Aloud" to hear the deal summary read clearly using your browser's native voice.
